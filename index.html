How it works

1

Prepare your spreadsheet

Download the template or the example, and fill it in with your Pareto-optimal solutions, one per row. Add each column's unit in parentheses after its name.

2

Upload your spreadsheet

Upload it to the tool, then mark which columns are objectives (minimize or maximize) and which are design parameters.

3

Set your priorities

Adjust simple sliders to say how much each objective matters to you.

4

Explore the results

See the ranked solutions, the changes each one involves, and charts that show the trade-offs.

5

Download your results

Save any of the charts as a PNG or SVG image, the ranked solutions as an Excel file, and the scenario comparison as an Excel file or image.

Your data stays private: This tool does all of its work directly inside your web browser. That means your spreadsheet is never sent to, or stored on, any outside server. Closing the page clears everything.
